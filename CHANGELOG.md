# Changelog

## 0.4.1 Alpha
- Extend italic i/l baseline exits to reduce the white interval before following letters, with unchanged 600-unit advances.
- Suppress native i/j dots before supported combining top marks, including after intervening below marks.
- Add repeatable light/dark optical proofs at 12–20 px and a before/after overview.
- Rebuild all eight faces, combined TTC, embedded previews, and marketing specimens.
- Upright core outlines remain unchanged after review; native-platform and language-specific caron review remain open.

## 0.4.0 Alpha
- Public family renamed from Bearing Mono to Damonic.
- Original ASCII design retained from 0.3.4.
- Release engineering targets: complete declared Latin coverage, improved combining-mark positioning, corrected embedding metadata and deterministic builds.
- Full pinned Nerd Fonts Mono edition and combined eight-face TTC.
- Standalone preview, editable source package, compatibility TTFs and web fonts.

See generated verification reports for measured results and remaining limitations.
